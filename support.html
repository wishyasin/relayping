Support

Need help, found a bug, or want a feature? Open a request or email relayping.support@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days (Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 Istanbul time, UTC+3). Please do not paste webhook URLs in public requests: they contain a secret token.

Common questions

The test message does not arrive

Check that the webhook URL starts with https://chat.googleapis.com/v1/spaces/ and that your Google Workspace admin allows incoming webhooks. Webhooks need a Google Workspace Business or Enterprise account.

I get too many notifications

Edit the rule: limit it to certain projects, remove events, or set target statuses (for example “Done”) so only those status changes are sent.

Can each issue have its own thread?

Yes. “Keep all updates of an issue in one thread” is on by default.

Can I send direct messages to individual people?

Not yet. Messages go to Google Chat spaces through webhooks. Personal notifications are on our roadmap.

RelayPing is an independent app and is not affiliated with Google or Atlassian.
