For Jira Cloud teams on Google Workspace

The Jira updates that matter, in the right Google Chat space.

RelayPing connects Jira Cloud to Google Chat. Pick the projects, events and statuses you care about, and each issue's updates stay together in one thread instead of flooding the room.

Rules, not noise

Choose projects and events: issue created, assignee changed, status changed, new comment, mentions.

Only the statuses you need

Notify a space only when work reaches “In Review” or “Done”, for example.

One thread per issue

Every update of an issue lands in the same Google Chat thread, so conversations stay readable.

Two-minute setup

Create a webhook in your Google Chat space, paste it into RelayPing in Jira settings, send a test message. Done.

How to set it up

  1. In Google Chat, open a space → Apps & integrations → Webhooks → Add webhook, and copy the URL.
  2. In Jira, go to Settings → Apps → Google Chat Notifications and click Add rule.
  3. Paste the URL, choose projects and events, and click Send test message.

Google Chat webhooks require a Google Workspace Business or Enterprise account, and your Workspace admin must allow incoming webhooks.
